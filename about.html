ABOUT THE STUDIO

Technology should feel helpful.

A1AppsStudio is an Android development brand creating mobile applications, practical utilities, and games for a wide range of everyday needs.

✦

Our purpose

We turn ideas into approachable mobile experiences. Each project begins with a clear use case and aims to make its core tasks understandable and convenient.

◇

How we work

We focus on useful features, readable interfaces, dependable behavior, and ongoing improvements. The exact design and technology choices depend on each release.

♧

Privacy with context

Applications can differ in features, permissions, data handling, and service providers. We provide app-specific privacy disclosures so people can review relevant details for the app they use.

⌁

Our scope

Our work spans tools, productivity, media, file workflows, learning, entertainment, photography, lifestyle, and mobile games. Areas of work may change as projects evolve.

Developer identity

Our visual brand is A1AppsStudio. The Google Play developer name used for legal and privacy information is A1Appstudio.

We do not publish unverified contact, business registration, or location details. For enquiries, use the contact page.
