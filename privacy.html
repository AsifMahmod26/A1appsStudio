Developer and scope

Developer: A1Appstudio

This policy covers Android applications and games developed and published by A1Appstudio, including releases already available, releases under development, and future releases. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” mean A1Appstudio as the app developer and publisher.

Privacy practices are not identical across all apps. The features, data, permissions, SDKs, service providers, retention periods, and user controls for one app may differ from another and may change between versions. This policy describes the common framework; it does not claim that every listed data type or practice applies to every app.

For the app you use, read its app-specific privacy disclosure linked from its Google Play store listing and available within the app where provided. That disclosure must identify the app and explain its actual data practices, including relevant SDKs and services. If the app-specific disclosure conflicts with this general framework on an app-specific practice, follow the more specific disclosure for that app, subject to applicable law.

App-specific disclosures

Before an app is published or its data practices materially change, its disclosure should be reviewed against the actual release, code, permissions, and third-party integrations. Depending on the app, the disclosure should state:

  • What personal and sensitive information the app and its integrated SDKs collect, access, use, or share, and the purposes for doing so.
  • Whether information is processed on the device or sent to a server, who operates that server, and how long information is kept.
  • Any advertising, analytics, crash reporting, payment, sign-in, cloud, communication, or other service provider involved.
  • Permissions and device data used by each feature, whether access is optional, and how to revoke it.
  • Available controls for consent, access, correction, deletion, and account or data removal.
  • Whether the app works offline, includes online features, or transfers information across borders.

A shared policy URL does not replace an app’s accurate Google Play Data safety answers, in-app notices, consent flows, or any app-specific policy or supplement required by law or a platform. The publisher is responsible for keeping those materials consistent with the shipped app.

Information and data

The information an app handles depends on its features. Where relevant, an app may process one or more of the following categories. This is a list of possible categories, not a statement that every app collects them.

  • Information you provide: content, settings, feedback, support messages, or account details you choose to submit.
  • Files and media: documents, photos, video, audio, or other content you select or create using an app. Whether processing stays on-device or is transmitted must be explained in that app’s disclosure.
  • Device and app information: app version, device or operating system attributes, diagnostics, performance events, and crash details, if enabled by the app or its service providers.
  • Usage and interaction information: feature use or interaction events, if analytics or similar functionality is present and disclosed.
  • Network or approximate location information: connection details, IP address, or location data only where a feature or service uses them and the app-specific disclosure says so.
  • Purchase or advertising information: transaction status or advertising identifiers where relevant third-party services provide these functions.

Information may be generated by the app, supplied by you, provided by the device or operating system, or processed by a third-party service. Consult the disclosure for the relevant app to identify the actual sources and categories.

How information may be used

Depending on the app and its features, information may be used to provide a requested function; save your settings or content; operate an account or online feature; respond to support requests; maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the app or service; meet legal obligations; or provide analytics, advertising, payments, or other disclosed services. Each app-specific disclosure must identify the purposes that actually apply and the applicable legal basis or consent mechanism where required.

We do not use a purpose listed here as blanket permission to process information in every app. A purpose that does not apply to an app should not be represented as that app’s practice.

Permissions and device access

An app may request Android permissions when a feature needs access to a device capability or content. Examples can include camera, microphone, photos or videos, files, notifications, location, nearby devices, or network access. These are examples only; any app should request only permissions needed for its features and explain their use in context.

You can manage permissions in Android settings. If you deny or later revoke permission, the related feature may not work. Some content can instead be selected using Android system pickers, where supported. Review the app-specific disclosure and permission prompts for the actual access used by the app.

Sharing and service providers

An app may use third-party providers to deliver functions such as hosting, analytics, crash diagnostics, advertising, payments, account sign-in, maps, or media processing. Whether any provider receives information depends on that app’s implementation. The relevant app-specific disclosure should identify provider categories, purposes, and information involved. Providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Information may also be disclosed when necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect people or the integrity of a service, or support a business transfer where permitted by law. We do not claim that information is never shared; the app-specific disclosure must describe sharing that actually occurs.

Retention and deletion

Retention periods depend on the data, purpose, app, and applicable legal requirements. Data kept locally may remain until you remove it, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or use an available in-app deletion feature. Data sent to a service may be retained for a defined period or until a request is completed, as stated in that app’s disclosure and the provider’s terms.

Where an app offers an account or stores personal data on a server, its app-specific disclosure should explain how to request account and associated data deletion, including an appropriate web deletion route if required. Uninstalling an app does not necessarily delete data already held by a service provider. We do not promise a single retention period or deletion behavior for every app.

Security

Appropriate safeguards depend on the nature of the app and the information it handles. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. The app-specific disclosure should describe material security and storage practices at an appropriate level and identify relevant service providers where applicable.

Children and family audiences

Age audience and child-directed status are determined separately for each app. This general policy does not state that any particular app is directed to children or suitable for every age. Apps intended for children or mixed audiences must provide the notices, consent mechanisms, advertising restrictions, and data practices required by applicable child privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements. Refer to the relevant app’s store listing and specific disclosure for its audience.

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and the app, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or a copy of personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also be able to withdraw consent. The available rights, identity checks, response periods, and exceptions depend on applicable law and the relevant app’s data practices.

Use controls in the app or contact the developer using the contact route in the app-specific disclosure. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers, or sensitive files in an initial support message. We may need reasonable information to verify and respond to a request.

International processing

If an app or its service providers process information in another country, the app-specific disclosure should identify that possibility and any safeguards or transfer mechanisms required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, apps, services, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision to this page. A material change to an app’s actual data practices should also be reflected in that app’s disclosure and, where required, accompanied by notice or consent before the new practice begins.

Contact about privacy

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact details in the relevant app’s Google Play listing or in-app privacy information. A verified developer email must be added to this website and the applicable app disclosures before publication.
