About Us

A1Appstudio develops Android applications, tools, utilities, and mobile games. We respect user privacy and are committed to protecting personal information. This policy explains our general privacy practices.

Information Collection and Use

Depending on the application and its features, we may process personal information voluntarily provided by users, device information, diagnostics, local files, media, app settings, preferences, and game progress.

Information is used to provide application functionality, improve services, resolve technical issues, and respond to support requests.

Permissions

Our applications may request access to storage, camera, microphone, notifications, or other device features when required. Permissions depend on each application's functionality and can be managed through Android settings.

Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell personal information. Some applications may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, or other functions. Applicable data sharing must be disclosed in the relevant application's privacy information.

We use appropriate security measures to protect information, although no system can guarantee complete security.

Data Retention and User Rights

Data retention depends on the application and services used. Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information where applicable by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Our applications and games may serve different age groups. Applications intended for children must comply with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements.

App-Specific Privacy

Different applications may process different types of information. Each published application must provide accurate disclosures about its actual data practices, permissions, third-party services, and data deletion options.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our applications, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.
